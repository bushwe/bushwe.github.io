Wind Turbine Design & Optimization

A semester-long team project designing, building, and optimizing a working wind turbine prototype — converting wind energy into measurable electrical output. Over nine weeks we iterated on blade geometry, materials, gear ratios, and generator wiring, using an Arduino and MATLAB to record and analyze real power data at every stage.

Engineering Work

  • Built the turbine tower, rotor, and geartrain; wired the generator to an Arduino through a resistor network
  • Tested blade count, pitch angle, and shape — found 4 flat blades at a 20° pitch maximized power output
  • Compared balsa wood vs. cardboard blades to quantify how stiffness and mass affect performance
  • Tested gear ratios to trade off rotational speed against torque at the generator
  • Modeled rotor scaling and wind-speed effects in MATLAB to predict power and battery charging time

Problems Solved

  • Diagnosed a "dead" generator: it only produces power spinning counterclockwise — and found broken motor leads, re-wiring with direct alligator-clip connections
  • Calculated real-world efficiency by comparing theoretical wind power to measured electrical output
  • Improved measurement accuracy with repeated trials and averaged rotation timing
